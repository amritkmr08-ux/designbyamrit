The three modes

Every behaviour below hangs off one of three modes. A content control is in exactly one of them, and only one control can be past browse at a time.

ModeWhat the user seesEntered by
browseNothing, until hover. After 120 ms on a paragraph, its whole run shows a light grey box and a grey name pill — only that run, never two at once. Paragraphs that belong to no clause group the same way and say Uncategorised with a grey dot.Default. Also on Esc, or a click outside.
The format controls live in the toolbar permanently and dim when nothing is in editing, rather than appearing and disappearing — a toolbar that changes shape under the pointer is its own bug.
Hover preview keeps working in active — every control except the focused one still answers the pointer. editing switches it off, so only the control being edited is ever boxed.
activeWhite box with a solid 1px dark line, paragraphs filled #D9EBF7, the pill goes dark and gains a pencil. Right panel expands the matching clause row: category select, Prefered badge, occurrences, ⋮ and ✓.Click its row in the panel, or ✓ out of editing.
editingDashed teal border, text is editable, drag grips at both ends, format bar in the toolbar, chip turns into the yellow Editing badge with a ✓.Single click the text (caret lands where you clicked), or the pencil in the pill. ✓ or a click outside saves. While editing, no other control lights up on hover.

Boundary drag

  • Grips appear only in editing. Top grip moves the start, bottom grip moves the end.
  • Dragging onto an uncategorised paragraph extends the control. The boundary takes a paragraph only once the pointer is ~14 px inside it, so it follows the hand with a little weight. Release → chip shows Processing for ~1.7 s while the new paragraph washes in blue → solid border returns and the mapped total goes up.
  • Dragging onto a paragraph owned by another category is refused: border goes red-dashed and the error card appears at the grip. Release snaps back. Nothing is written.
  • The refusal is category-based, not position-based. Same category on both sides would merge; the design never shows that case, so it isn't built. See open questions.

Tags

  • Tags are quiet at rest. They show faintly when their section is hovered, and in their working yellow once it is selected or editing.
  • Hover a visible tag → amber outline plus a dark tooltip with the tag name.
  • Click a tag from any mode and the control goes to editing with the tag card open on that tag, in one gesture.
  • editing + select text → Search & add new tag. Flat list, no current value.
  • The tag card is Search with the current tag checked at the top, then ALL TAGS. Picking a different one replaces it.
  • Two menus, two different headers and two different list shapes. They are not the same component.

Clause split

  • editing + drag-select, then release → a card appears at the selection. Which card you get is decided by boundaries, not length: a clause is made of whole paragraphs, so a selection that reaches both ends of a paragraph (or spans several) gets the Clauses | Tags card; anything inside a paragraph can only be a tag. Trailing whitespace and punctuation are ignored.
  • Pick a category and that paragraph leaves its control and becomes its own.
  • Uncategorised is pinned to the bottom of the card, outside the scrolling list, because it is the way out of a clause rather than another category — and because the drag refusal sends the user here by name. The parent clause survives on whatever paragraphs are left; it only leaves the document when it has none, and its panel row falls back to Uncategorised.
  • Chip reads Tagged to — <category> until you commit with the ✓ or click outside.
  • The paragraphs left behind stay one clause but are now in two runs, so the parent draws as two boxes sharing one identity.

Open questions for engineering

What does "· 2" count?

"Termination Clause · 2" sits on a control spanning three paragraphs, and still reads · 2 after a drag adds a fourth. So it isn't paragraphs. Occurrences across documents? Variations? This prototype declares it on the clause and leaves it alone, because guessing would hide the question.

Split leaves a non-contiguous clause

The canvas note says the parent ends up with two variations (para 1 and para 3) and the user may keep or merge them. The Done frames only draw one box. This prototype draws both runs. Needs a merge affordance, which no frame covers.

Near-miss selections

A drag that covers most of a paragraph but stops a few words short gets the tag card, because it isn't a whole paragraph. That is the only rule the frames support. If users near-miss often in testing, the fix is one card carrying both tabs every time — not a looser threshold, which just moves the wrong guess somewhere else.

Undo

No frame shows undo for a drag, a split, or a retag. All three are destructive to a mapping. Decide whether Ctrl+Z is in scope or the ⋮ menu carries a revert.

Two people at once

Nothing defines what happens if another reviewer commits a category on the same clause while this one is in editing mode.

Tags inside a split

When paragraph 2 splits out, its tag goes with it. That is what this prototype does. Confirm tags follow the paragraph and not the clause.
