At Sirion, I make complexity feel calm.

Legal teams already face difficult decisions; the interface shouldn't make them harder.

The work is calm. The route here wasn’t — graphic design, fashion, a denim label I co-founded, then product. I prefer proving an idea in a browser, not a deck.

A career made of different materials

This is not a clean ladder upward. Each practice changed what I notice in the next one.

  • 2013 · NIFT ChennaiVisual design

    Composition is not decoration. It decides what gets seen first, what waits and what disappears. Left behind: hierarchy, typography, rhythm and an instinct for removing what weakens the whole.

  • 2013–2017 · Lee CooperFashion and material

    A beautiful direction still has to survive production, repetition and the hands that make it. Left behind: respect for constraints, systems that tolerate variation and the difference between a concept and a thing that ships.

  • 2017–2018 · Zhero DenimFounder reality

    Design cannot rescue a broken route to market. The whole system has to work. Left behind: commercial judgement, humility and a permanent suspicion of beautiful answers to the wrong problem.

  • 2019–2021 · AirtelConsumer scale

    At scale, one unclear choice becomes millions of moments of friction. Left behind: behavioural evidence, intent-led support and an understanding that small interface decisions compound.

  • 2021–now · Sirion.aiEnterprise AI

    AI can answer. A person still has to judge the evidence, consequence and next move. Left behind: decision systems, provenance, reversible action, human checkpoints and interfaces that remain calm when the model is uncertain.

  • Every day · FatherhoodWatching hesitation

    My daughter rarely explains where an experience failed. She pauses, tries something else, or leaves. Left behind: a sharper eye for where confidence breaks — and respect for what people do instead of what they say.
