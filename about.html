How I work now

  1. 01 · What do I believe?

    Hypothesis

    I sit with the people doing the work and write down what I think is true, before I draw anything.

  2. 02 · Make it real

    Build

    A working prototype in HTML, built with AI tools, so people react to the real thing instead of a picture of it.

  3. 03 · What happens?

    Test

    The people who do the work try it on their own tasks. What they do counts for more than what they say.

  4. 04 · Change it

    Iterate

    Change the solution, not the slide. Then start the next round.

This idea was picked for UX India 2023

My concept for AI-assisted contract redlining and review was selected for a Lightning Talk at UX India 2023 in Bengaluru. It pointed reviewers to the risky parts of a contract, suggested fixes, and drafted replacement clauses. The initial concept and the talk were mine; I didn’t work on the version that later shipped.
