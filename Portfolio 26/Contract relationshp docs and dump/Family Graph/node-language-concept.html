Group vs record — node visual language

On canvas today a bucket holding 145 contracts and a single contract are drawn the same way. The eye has no way to tell scaffolding from substance.

A group is a question. A record is a thing. A lens bucket has no ID, no paper, no signature, and disappears the moment you switch lens. A record is a legal instrument that exists whether you are looking at it or not. They should not share a silhouette.

Today

Same radius, same fill, same stroke, same left stripe, same type, same weight, near-identical box. The only difference is that one ends in a number and the other has a second line — a distinction you have to read rather than see.

Group · a bucket of 145

Reads as: a card. Same object class as the thing below it.

Record · one contract

Reads as: a card. Nothing says this one has paper behind it.

Four cues, working at four distances

No single cue survives every zoom level, so they stack. Shape carries at 40%, marker and chevron at 70%, typeface only up close. At any zoom at least one is doing the work.

Proposed

Group · lens bucket

Capsule, dot, sans, chevron, count in mono. Nothing to open in the panel — a cohort owns no paper.

Record · contract

Card, status bar, mono code, name beneath. Has documents, dates, parties — a panel with substance.

capsule = a set card = an instrument dot = dimension colour bar = record status

The case that proves it — one amendment, two presentations

Amendment is the only dimension that is both a lens and a record. Grouped, it is a bucket of SOWs. Selected off a SOW, it is a legal instrument with its own paper. Same underlying amendment — and now you can never mistake which one is on screen.

As a lens bucket · Group by amendment

Capsule. No code — a bucket has no ID. Carries its legal state and what it affects.

As a record · satellite off SOW-003

Card. Mono code, because this one you cite. Dashed edge in, because it amends.

In situ — where lens ends and structure begins

Toggle it. The point is not that either looks better in isolation — it is that in the proposed strip your eye finds the seam between column 3 and column 4 without reading a single word.

Does it survive zoom

At 45% the label is gone and the type is unreadable — but capsule-versus-card still separates the two classes. That is the cue doing the load-bearing work on a wide view.

What this does not change

Colour semantics stay exactly as they are — status colours on records, dimension colours on buckets. Edge grammar stays: solid governs, dashed amends.

Selected and open states stay: white 1.5px stroke when selected, dimension-coloured stroke when the bucket is the open path.

Considered and dropped

Stacked-card shadow behind groups — the most literal “this is many” cue, but the 3–6px of offset eats into the 12px vertical gap between siblings and the column starts to look crowded at 30 records.

Outline-only groups — legible, but they vanish against the canvas at low zoom, which is exactly where the cue is needed most.
