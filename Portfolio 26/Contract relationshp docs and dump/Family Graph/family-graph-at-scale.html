What the lines mean
governs · issued under
amends · modifies

Solid lines are real parentage. Dashed lines are modifier records — an amendment or a change order attaches to something, it is not a level beneath it.

Group by is a lens for handling volume. It re-cuts the same 340 SOWs and never re-parents them.

What the right panel answers

Select a group and it answers for the cohort — combined value, what needs action, which amendments touch it. A cohort owns no paper, so it has no Documents card.

Select a record and you get its details plus its Documents: executed, exhibits, drafts, correspondence. Clicking any row in the panel lights the matching nodes on the canvas.
