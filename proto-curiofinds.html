About

Better thinking. One idea at a time.

Small, sourced finds on design, research and product. Each one leads with the surprise, and links to its proof.

What this prototype changes from the live site
  • Opening an idea expands the card in place to the full row. Cards after it move up to fill the gap. The live site goes to a separate page.
  • The expanded card keeps the live detail format: the idea, in practice, a common mistake, when to use it, why it matters, the source, and Follow the thought.
  • Wall cards follow the top-aligned grid file: one title size and weight for every card, and each card is exactly as tall as its own text. Gaps stay 16px in both directions.
  • Card colour cycles in a fixed order, as in that file. It doesn’t follow category.
  • The ↗ arrow is removed; “Explore idea →” and the save heart stay.
  • The wall loads 24 cards at a time as you scroll. Search and chips look through all ideas, loaded or not.
  • New: Surprise me, a “Read” marker, Shelves and Trail layouts, a zero-result block, and search that ignores apostrophes and plural s.
  • Follow the thought carries a reason line. About 25 links have hand-written reasons; the rest say “Also in [category]”.
  • The 400 view repeats the real ideas to simulate scale. Repeats are marked.
  • Concept text is written for this prototype and hasn’t been editor-checked. Sources are shown only where a real page is known; the rest say a source is still to be added. The synthetic users finding uses the live page’s text.
