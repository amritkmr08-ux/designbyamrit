## Strategy decisions (locked)
- **Two case studies only** — depth over breadth. Craft gap closed *inside* them, not via a 3rd case.
- **Target: Staff / Lead only.** At 6 years this is ambitious → referrals are the main channel.
  Portfolio must show scope and influence, not just craft: direction-setting, cross-team reach,
  frameworks others adopted.
- **No invented metrics, ever.** Rejected ChatGPT's suggested "10+ years", "10K+ users",
  "20% faster review" — all false for this work. Pre-launch stays pre-launch.
